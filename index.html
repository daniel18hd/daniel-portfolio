01 — TONGO · DATA PLATFORM · 2026

The Revenue Engine

Production Postgres→ Read-only replica→ Snowflake warehouse→ Unified model (+ SVB & Astra txns + admin events)→ Analytics front-end for every team
The problem

The company's truth was scattered: product data in Postgres, money moving through Silicon Valley Bank and Astra, operational events in the admin. Nobody could answer "where is revenue coming from and where are we stuck?" without a day of manual work.

What I built
  • A read-only replica of the production Postgres, mirrored into Snowflake — analytics power with zero risk to prod.
  • Unified every data source in one warehouse: product transactions, SVB and Astra banking movements, admin events.
  • A metric layer with shared definitions, so every team argues from the same numbers.
  • A new front-end that translates the data for the whole org — no SQL required to understand the business.
What it unlocked
  • Growth patterns and revenue concentration visible in real time.
  • Opportunity detection: where revenue lives, where it's leaking.
  • Underwriting bottlenecks surfaced — where applications get stuck in the pipeline, and why.
  • Historical patterns and seasonality for pricing and revenue-share decisions.
  • Replaced spreadsheets as the company's operating tool: founders open it every morning.
My role

End to end: architecture decisions with engineering, the SQL and metric definitions, the visual design of every view, and weekly iteration with founders and sales on what each screen needed to answer.

02 — TONGO · PRODUCT REBUILD · 2026

Fintech, Rebuilt

Legacy product→ Market discovery→ New product thesis→ Rebuild from zero→ New web + mobile app
The problem

The existing application carried years of legacy decisions — an approach that no longer matched what modern fintech users expect, and an architecture that made every new feature expensive.

The process
  • Discovery across the fintech apps that set the bar today — what earns their valuations, what users actually praise, which patterns are table stakes.
  • Defined a new product thesis: a modern-fintech approach for commission-based earners, not a lift-and-shift of the old app.
  • Rebuilt from zero: new web version and new mobile app, designed and shipped as one coherent experience.
Why it matters
  • A 360° turn on what the company offers today — the new flagship, not an iteration.
  • Web + app parity from day one.
  • Built on the Revenue Engine's data layer: the product and its analytics share one source of truth.
My role

Led the discovery, wrote the product thesis, prototyped flows with AI tooling (Claude Code, Cursor) before committing engineering time, and drove the rebuild with the founding team.
